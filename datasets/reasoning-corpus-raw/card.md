# reasoning-corpus-raw

Raw text split for the `reasoning-corpus` release.

## Provenance

This split is a filtered extract of the FineMath-3+ mathematical web corpus
published by the HuggingFaceTB team. Documents were selected for clear
step-by-step mathematical explanations, boilerplate was removed, and duplicates
were dropped. No synthetic text was added to this split.

## Contents

- `data/part-0001.parquet` — normalized document text and source metadata.
